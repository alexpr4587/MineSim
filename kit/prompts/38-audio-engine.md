# Prompt 38 of 46 (v0.5): audio engine and procedural synthesis

Follow AGENTS.md. Goal: a small audio engine for sound effects and background music, with no audio files in the repository: everything is synthesized by code (this also keeps the licensing clean). First read ui/app.py, ui/theme.py, core/events.py and tests/test_architecture.py.

1. New package src/minesim/audio/. It may import PySide6 and numpy; core/, scripting/ and runner/ must never import it (extend tests/test_architecture.py and AGENTS.md). ui/ imports it.
2. audio/synth.py (numpy only, deterministic, float32, 44100 Hz): oscillators (sine, triangle, soft saw), ADSR envelopes, one-pole low-pass and high-pass filters, seeded noise, echo and a light reverb (a few comb filters), fade in and out, stereo panning, mixing with offsets, normalization to a peak of about -8 dBFS, int16 conversion and WAV writing. The sound character is soft and warm: no energy above about 6 kHz and attacks of at least 2 ms.
3. audio/recipes.py: reads content/audio_sfx.json, a list of {id, kind, params, variants, pitch_jitter, gain}, and builds the buffers. Kinds: thunk (a sine with a fast pitch drop plus low-passed noise), pluck (a triangle with a soft attack and exponential decay), bell (inharmonic partials with different decays), chime (a short arpeggio of bells), coin (two quick sines plus a tiny sparkle), click, whoosh (noise with a sweeping low-pass), pad (detuned triangles with slow attack and release), drip, hum, crackle. Variants use seeded pitch and timing jitter so repeated sounds do not feel mechanical. Put a few example entries in audio_sfx.json (ui_click, mine_thunk, coin, chime_achievement, drip); prompt 39 adds the full list.
4. audio/music.py: generative background music. content/audio_music.json defines moods {id, root_note, scale, bpm, chords (scale degrees), pad_gain, melody_density, bass, brightness}; render_loop(mood, seed, bars=8) builds a stereo loop: a slow pad on the chords, a sparse plucked melody on the scale (a random walk with long rests), an occasional soft bass note and a gentle echo. The loop must be seamless: render one extra bar, fold the tail onto the beginning and verify there is no click at the boundary. Moods: surface (major pentatonic, about 72 bpm, warm), galleries (dorian, 64), deep (minor pentatonic, 56, hollow), abyss (very sparse, 48, low).
5. audio/backend.py: interface AudioBackend (play_sfx(sound_id, volume, pan), play_music(track_id, fade_ms), stop_music(fade_ms), play_ambience(loop_id, volume, fade_ms), set_volumes(master, music, sfx), available). Implementations: NullBackend (records the calls; used by the tests) and QtBackend (QtMultimedia: a pool of QSoundEffect objects per sound for overlapping SFX; QMediaPlayer plus QAudioOutput for music with a crossfade between two players). The backend is chosen automatically: Null when the environment variable MINESIM_AUDIO is off, when the Qt platform is offscreen (unless MINESIM_AUDIO=on) or when no audio output device exists.
6. audio/cache.py: render all SFX variants and music loops to WAV files under the platformdirs cache folder (audio/<hash of the recipes and the code version>/); regenerate when the recipes change; do the rendering in a background QThread at startup and start the sound when it is ready, so the game never blocks. Overrides: files in <user data>/audio/override/<id>.wav (SFX) and music/<mood>.(wav|ogg|mp3) replace the synthesized sounds.
7. audio/manager.py: AudioManager(settings, backend, cache) with play(sound_id, volume=1.0, pan=0.0), set_mood(mood, fade_ms), duck(amount, ms), and protections from content/audio.json: max_voices 12 and min_interval_ms per sound id, so 20 miners at 20x speed cannot flood the output.
8. AudioSettings (master 0.8, music 0.25, sfx 0.6, music_enabled, sfx_enabled, muted) persisted with QSettings.
9. CLI: --selftest-audio renders one SFX and one music loop into a temporary folder, runs the manager with the NullBackend and exits 0.
10. tools/render_audio.py <sound_id or mood> writes a WAV into artifacts/ so every sound can be reviewed by ear.
11. Tests: the synthesis is finite, within [-1, 1], deterministic for a seed and has the expected lengths; loops have no click at the boundary (compare the first and the last samples); recipe validation errors name the file and the id; the manager applies the voice cap, the interval limit, ducking and the volume math with the NullBackend; settings persist; cache keys change when a recipe changes; the backend selection rules.

ACCEPTANCE: uv run minesim --selftest-audio prints OK on Linux and Windows CI (no audio device needed) and tools/render_audio.py produces listenable WAV files.
COMMIT: feat(audio): procedural synth, music generator and audio engine
